# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead oversees testing strategies, establishes quality standards, and ensures deliverables meet defined quality criteria. They coordinate testing efforts across the team and maintain quality metrics throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and implement testing strategies aligned with project scope and timelines
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for all deliverables
- Coordinate testing efforts and manage the QA team's workload
- Identify, document, and track quality issues and defects
- Establish and monitor quality metrics and KPIs
- Participate in design reviews to identify potential quality risks early

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test planning, reviews code quality, provides feedback on defects and quality concerns
- **Reports to Project Managers**: Delivers quality status updates, escalates quality risks, and provides input on timeline adjustments needed for testing
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Ensures quality standards align with product requirements and customer expectations
- **Communicates with Stakeholders**: Provides quality assessments and addresses quality-related concerns

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet defined quality standards before release
- Identify and mitigate quality risks early in the project lifecycle
- Maintain high stakeholder confidence in product quality
- Continuously improve testing processes and efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Quality reports and test execution summaries
- Defect logs and issue tracking
- Quality metrics dashboards
- Participation in planning meetings for QA strategy alignment

---

## Change Management Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Change Management Coordinator manages change requests, assesses their impact on project scope, timeline, and resources, and coordinates approvals. They communicate changes to all affected stakeholders and ensure smooth implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and maintain change management processes and procedures
- Receive, document, and evaluate change requests
- Assess impact of proposed changes on project scope, timeline, budget, and resources
- Coordinate change request reviews and obtain necessary approvals
- Communicate approved changes to all affected teams and stakeholders
- Track implementation of approved changes
- Maintain change logs and documentation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Supports Project Managers**: Acts as the primary change coordinator; escalates critical changes and provides impact assessments
- **Coordinates with Developers**: Communicates scope changes and ensures technical team understands implementation requirements
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Validates that changes align with product strategy and business objectives
- **Liaises with Stakeholders**: Communicates change status, impact, and timeline to all stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize uncontrolled scope creep and project disruptions
- Ensure transparent communication about all project changes
- Enable data-driven change decisions based on impact assessments
- Maintain project alignment with original objectives and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Change request forms and impact assessments
- Change approval meeting minutes
- Stakeholder change notifications
- Change logs and status reports

## Risk Owner / Risk Manager

### Role Summary
The Risk Owner/Manager identifies potential risks to project success, develops mitigation strategies, monitors risk indicators, and escalates risks when necessary. They help the team proactively address challenges before they become critical issues.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document risks throughout the project lifecycle
- Assess risk probability and impact
- Develop mitigation strategies and contingency plans for high-priority risks
- Assign risk owners and track mitigation progress
- Monitor risk indicators and warning signs
- Escalate critical risks to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Facilitate risk reviews and retrospectives to capture lessons learned
- Maintain and update risk registers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Supports Project Managers**: Provides risk assessments and works with PM on mitigation planning and escalation decisions
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Identifies technical risks and gathers input on feasibility and technical constraints
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Assesses business and market risks related to product decisions
- **Reports to Leadership/Stakeholders**: Escalates critical risks and provides risk status updates

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions by identifying and mitigating risks early
- Enable informed decision-making through comprehensive risk analysis
- Build team confidence through proactive risk management
- Capture organizational learning from project risks and mitigation approaches

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessments
- Mitigation strategy proposals
- Risk status updates and escalation notices
- Retrospective reports and lessons learned documentation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect defines the overall technical approach, reviews critical technical decisions, ensures architectural consistency, and guides the development team through technical execution. They bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design approach aligned with project requirements
- Review and approve critical technical decisions and designs
- Ensure architectural consistency and adherence to technical standards
- Guide Developers through complex technical implementation
- Identify and communicate technical risks and constraints
- Mentor and support the development team on technical matters
- Participate in design reviews and code reviews for critical components
- Document technical decisions and architectural rationale

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews designs and code, mentors on best practices
- **Advises Project Managers**: Communicates technical feasibility, timeline impact of technical decisions, and technical risks
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Translates product requirements into technical approaches and identifies trade-offs
- **Collaborates with QA Lead**: Reviews test strategies for technical completeness and provides input on test coverage

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Build team technical capability and knowledge
- Enable informed trade-offs between technical and business considerations

### Typical Communication
- Technical architecture documents and design proposals
- Technical decision records (ADRs)
- Architecture review meeting participation
- Technical risk assessments and recommendations

---

## Communications Manager

### Role Summary
The Communications Manager develops and executes communication plans, manages stakeholder updates, ensures consistent messaging across all project communications, and documents project progress. They keep all stakeholders informed and aligned throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive communication plans aligned with project phases
- Identify stakeholder groups and define communication needs for each group
- Create and distribute regular project status reports and updates
- Prepare meeting agendas, minutes, and key decision documentation
- Coordinate and support project meetings and presentations
- Manage project documentation and knowledge repositories
- Ensure consistent and clear messaging across all project communications
- Address stakeholder questions and concerns
- Document project progress and milestones for organizational records

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Managers**: Provides communication support and ensures alignment on messaging and updates
- **Collaborates with all team members**: Gathers information from Developers, QA, Technical Leads, and other roles for comprehensive status updates
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Communicates product strategy and customer-facing updates
- **Engages with Stakeholders**: Serves as key point of contact for consistent, regular project communication

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and transparency throughout the project
- Reduce confusion and miscommunication across project teams
- Create comprehensive project documentation for future reference
- Build stakeholder confidence through clear, timely, and consistent communication

### Typical Communication
- Weekly/bi-weekly status reports and dashboards
- Project meeting minutes and decision logs
- Stakeholder briefings and presentations
- Project documentation and knowledge base entries
- Announcement and update communications
